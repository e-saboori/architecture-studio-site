About

Architecture shaped by delivery, detail, and calm coordination.

With 17 years across complex projects, I bring design sensitivity together with technical leadership.

My background spans architectural design development, documentation, coordination, and team leadership across residential, institutional, educational, assembly, sports arena, and mixed-use commercial projects in Iran, Venezuela, Belarus, Canada, and the United States.

Profile

From concept direction to construction documentation.

I am an OAA professional with a Master of Architecture from SBU in Tehran and a Master of Engineering in Construction Management from Concordia University in Montreal.

My work focuses on translating complex project requirements into coordinated, buildable architectural packages. I manage consultant and stakeholder coordination at both strategic and technical levels, mark up and guide drawing sets, develop architectural details, and lead teams through schematic design, design development, IFC, and construction documentation.

I also bring project management experience across client communication, budgets, schedules, reporting, internal coordination, and milestone delivery.
